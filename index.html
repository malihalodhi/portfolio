Hi, I'm Maliha

Before I ran campaigns for other people's brands, I built one of my own.

I scaled a fashion label across three countries while I was still a student, with barely any budget, and I built it around a few things I wasn't willing to compromise on. Clothes wrapped in paper and cloth instead of plastic. Stitching done by women from rural areas who weren't otherwise allowed to work outside their homes. A campaign shot with transgender models at a time when that wasn't a safe thing to do.

I brought that same instinct into agency work, running data backed campaigns for 14+ UK clients, and into an MSc dissertation on how a brand like Haribo could sell to toddlers without breaking the rules or the brand.

The Why Behind My Work 🎯

I believe marketing works best when it earns trust rather than manufactures it.

Whether through honest packaging, responsible sourcing, or showing customers real numbers instead of just telling a good story, brands grow stronger when they build genuine connections with the people they serve.

I build and run campaigns that are measurable and accountable at every stage.

From building a fashion brand from the ground up to managing client campaigns and performance dashboards, I focus on making sure every marketing decision has a clear purpose and every investment has a visible impact.
